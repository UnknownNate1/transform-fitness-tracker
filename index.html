This is a general-purpose tracker, not medical advice or a physical therapy program. It does not assess transfer safety, balance, joint limitations, or medical conditions. Because you said your left arm cannot be used and you sometimes use your legs to help with transfers, the starter session avoids left-arm exercises and floor transfers. Only do movements you already know you can perform safely and independently. Consider getting individualized guidance from a qualified clinician or physical therapist when accessible.
